💡 AI Daily Seller Tips & Growth Strategies

Product Strategies

  • Bundle complementary apparel designs to raise average order value (AOV).
  • Add oversized heavyweight tee options — currently dominating organic search.
  • Refresh mockups with lifestyle context photos instead of flat-lays.
  • Focus on embroidery and vintage fade aesthetics for premium margins.

YouTube & Shorts Tactics

  • Show product printing & texture in 15–30 second shorts.
  • Put high-intent keywords in the first 2 lines of video descriptions.
  • Maintain regular weekly uploads to train algorithmic retention.
  • Link your connected store directly via pinned comments.

Instagram Reels & Stories

  • Post reels showcasing BTS packing and printing processes.
  • Combine 5-8 focused niche tags rather than bloated tags.
  • Run interactive story polls for upcoming graphic drops.
  • Link directly to checkout via Instagram Product tagging.
