📖 Seller Playbook & Store Growth Tactics

Battle-tested strategies for scaling Print-on-Demand merchandise stores.

Product & Pricing

  • Bundle complementary apparel designs to push average order value above $35.
  • Add oversized heavyweight tee options — currently dominating search trends.
  • Refresh mockups with natural lifestyle drape instead of flat-lays.
  • Price with room for seasonal 20% discounts while retaining healthy margins.

Short-Form Video

  • Film 15-second fabric feel and print texture closeups for Shorts.
  • Keep video title under 50 characters with exact product niche keyword.
  • Maintain regular uploads: 3 focused shorts per week builds retention.
  • Pin your direct store checkout link in the comments.

Social & Community

  • Post Reels showing behind-the-scenes packing and shipping routines.
  • Combine 5-8 focused niche tags rather than generic flooded ones.
  • Run Story polls asking followers to vote on upcoming graphic drops.
  • Direct message repeat buyers with personalized appreciation perks.
