📖 Seller Playbook & Store Growth Tactics

Battle-tested strategies for Print-on-Demand scaling.

Product & Pricing

  • Bundle matching items to push average order value above $35.
  • Heavyweight oversized tees convert best on Etsy & TikTok Shop right now.
  • Use natural mockup photography showing real apparel drape.
  • Price with room for seasonal 20% sales while keeping 40%+ margin.

Short-Form Video

  • Film 15-second fabric feel and print quality closeups.
  • Keep title under 50 characters with the exact product niche keyword.
  • Consistency wins: 3 clean Shorts a week outperforms sporadic posting.
  • Pin your shop link as the top comment on every upload.

Social & Community

  • Show order packing and behind-the-scenes store operations.
  • Use 5-7 targeted niche hashtags instead of generic ones.
  • Run Story polls asking followers to vote on upcoming design drops.
  • Direct message repeat customers with personalized thank-you perks.
