Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Sellytics shows you your store's numbers and emails you a digest (daily or on demand). This page explains, in plain language, what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Your email address — it is your login, and it is where we send the digest email.
  • Your Supabase-managed login — your account and session data is stored and handled by Supabase, our sign-in provider.
  • Your Printify API token — stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Only this server can decrypt it, and only while making Printify requests on your behalf.
  • Store stats — such as your recent order count. These are fetched from Printify when you ask for them and are not stored long-term. Your shop name and shop id are saved with your connection so incoming webhooks can be matched to your account.
  • Order feed data — when you enable live order updates, we store order summaries from Printify (order id, status, tracking number, carrier, total, currency and a sanitised raw payload). This is so we can show your Live Orders list and send issue alerts.
  • Push subscription — if you turn on browser notifications, we store the push endpoint and keys from your browser. You can turn this off at any time in your browser settings.
  • Abuse-prevention data — when you use the contact form, we store a keyed hash of your IP address in a short-lived rate-limit counter (about one hour). We do not store the raw IP for this.
  • Server logs — request ID, route, status, error details and, for security, IP address. Logs never include message bodies, passwords, tokens or encryption keys.

Why we collect it

To show your dashboard, run the live order feed, send issue alerts you asked for, cancel an order when you click the cancel button, and send you the digest email. Nothing else. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and this site sets no analytics cookies.

Who processes it

We use the following service providers as data processors. They only process data on our instructions and for the purposes described here.

  • Supabase — database, authentication, order feed storage, push subscriptions and short-lived rate-limit counters. Account data, encrypted store tokens and your saved order statuses are stored there.
  • Netlify — hosting and running the API. Requests pass through Netlify’s servers.
  • Printify — the print-on-demand platform you connect. We send your API token to Printify to read your shop and order data, register webhooks, and, if you use the cancel button, request a cancellation. Printify processes that data under its own terms.
  • OpenRouter — AI tips. We send a prompt to OpenRouter to generate the daily tips. The prompt may include shop-related context (for example shop name and recent order count); today the daily tip uses only the date and season. We never send buyer names, addresses, emails or payment details to OpenRouter.
  • Gmail SMTP — email delivery.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We only share what is needed for the service to work, and we do not send buyer personal data to the AI provider.

How long we keep it

Your account, connection details, encrypted token, saved order feed and push subscription are kept until you delete them. Contact rate-limit counters reset after about one hour, and stale rows are removed automatically within a day. Live-fetched order counts are not stored long-term. When you delete your account, your data is removed.

Your rights (GDPR)

  • Access — ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Deletion — delete your account and data yourself from the dashboard, or ask us and we will do it.
  • Export — ask us for your data in a portable format.
  • Withdrawing consent — delete your account or contact us to stop processing.
  • Complaint — you may contact a data protection authority at any time.
