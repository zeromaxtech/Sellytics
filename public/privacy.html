Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Sellytics shows you your store's numbers and emails you a weekly digest. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Your email address — it is your login, and it is where we send the digest email.
  • Your Supabase-managed login — your account and session data is stored and handled by Supabase, our sign-in provider.
  • Your Printify API token — stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Only this server can decrypt it, and only while making Printify requests on your behalf.
  • Store stats, fetched live — such as your shop name and recent order count. These are fetched from Printify when you ask for them and are not stored long-term.

Why we collect it

Only to show your dashboard and to send you the digest email. Nothing else. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and this site sets no analytics cookies.

Who processes it

  • Supabase — database and authentication.
  • Netlify — hosting.
  • Gmail SMTP — email delivery.
  • OpenRouter — generates the generic daily tips. Only the current date and season are sent to OpenRouter; no user data is ever sent.

How long we keep it

Your account and encrypted token are kept until you delete them. Live-fetched store stats are not stored long-term. When you delete your account, your data is removed.

Your rights (GDPR)

  • Access — ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Deletion — delete your account and data yourself from the dashboard, or ask us and we will do it.
  • Export — ask us for your data in a portable format.
  • Withdrawing consent — delete your account or contact us to stop processing.
  • Complaint — you may contact a data protection authority at any time.
